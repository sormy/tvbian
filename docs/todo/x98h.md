# X98H: what is left

## 👤 By hand

One step each for an owner. Boot the image from an SD card (the eMMC's Android stays as it is) and
post the output on the pull request.

- [ ] Boot from the SD card and send `dmesg` and `systemctl --failed`.
- [ ] The CPU's speed bin: `xxd -s 0x00 -l 0x30 /sys/bus/nvmem/devices/sunxi-sid0/nvmem`.
- [ ] The pin bank voltages: `devmem 0x0300b348` (`PIO_POW_VAL`).
- [ ] The DRAM strap pins: `cat /sys/kernel/debug/gpio | grep -E 'PI1[4-6]'`.
- [ ] The remote: run `sudo evtest /dev/input/ir-remote`, press every button except power once, top
      to bottom, and send the log. It checks the 27 mapped keys and gives the codes for APP, KD and
      the cog.
- [ ] The panel's icons, lit one at a time. Stock's driver puts USB at 0, card at 2, Wi-Fi at 4 or
      6, APPS at 10 and Ethernet at 12; the PR owner's panel lit USB at 4 and card at 6.
- [ ] The `DRAM_VCC set to … mv` line from boot0, from a serial or stock log.
- [ ] PG19's level while Bluetooth is up: `grep -i pg19 /sys/kernel/debug/gpio`.
- [ ] The LEDs' colours.
- [ ] Which USB-A socket is OTG: a stick in each socket in turn, `lsusb -t` each time; the socket on
      the SoC's port 0 (`ehci0`/`ohci0`) is OTG. The side sockets are printed `USB2` and `USB3`, so
      the back one is the likely candidate.
- [ ] That the toothpick button is PH9: hold it at power-on, a cable in the OTG socket; the host
      should see a FEL device.
- [ ] The serial pads marked on `pcb-top.webp`, with the square one shown.
- [ ] Bluetooth classic or LE only: `btmgmt info`.
- [ ] Where the IR receiver is, and a photo of the sticker (`label.webp`).
- [ ] A seller or vendor page whose photos show this PCB or its ports, for `BOARD_HOME`.

## Unattended

- [ ] Stock's CPU speed bins 5 and 6: their own bin in `sun50i-cpufreq-nvmem`, then their voltages
      in the tree.
- [ ] The front panel: TM16xx's 3-wire SPI support built for `sunxi64`, then a `spi-gpio` node with
      the panel's digit map.
- [ ] A sound card for the S/PDIF output, and whether the I2S2 card is the radio's audio link.
- [ ] Bluetooth host wake on PG16, and Wi-Fi host wake on PG15 for wake-on-WLAN.
- [ ] Wi-Fi at a UHS speed: stock runs the SDIO bus at SDR104, ours at 50 MHz high speed, since
      mainline's `sunxi-mmc` has no tuning. Measure SDR50 on a box first.
