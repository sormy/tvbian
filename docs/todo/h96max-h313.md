# H96 Max H313: what is left

## Needs the owner

- [ ] USB 2.0 at 480M: a stick in each USB-A port in turn, then the port nearest the SD slot (OTG)
      as a host; `fio` measures each.
- [ ] The remote: press every button against `rc-h96max`'s 22 keys.
- [ ] The BLE remote: pair it with `pair-remote`, then check it reconnects after a reboot.
- [ ] The LED's blue and red: which pin drives each, then the colour on, off and in `s2idle`.
- [ ] SD hotplug: a card out and back in while running.
- [ ] Composite on 6.18: PAL colour bars on the AV jack, as on 7.3. The registers match 7.3's
      working state; nobody has watched the picture.
- [ ] Whether the FEL button in the AV jack reaches Linux: watch PH9, the factory tree's
      `recovery_key`, with `gpiomon` while it is pressed. If it does, it becomes `KEY_VENDOR` on
      `/dev/input/recovery-button`; the board tree has no key now.
- [ ] Install to the eMMC, on the owner's go-ahead: the box runs from the SD today.

## Unattended

- [ ] On 7.3, what was run on 6.18 only: ten warm reboots, eMMC and SD speed, `s2idle` 20 s, an
      all-CPU lockup, glmark2 on screen, the input names.
- [ ] Read the backup's eMMC back through the factory-settings FEL loader and match it.
- [ ] Heat: four cores trip at 70 °C and settle at 1200–1320 MHz, 73 °C peak; compare stock's trip
      points and cooling maps.
- [ ] The Wi-Fi's SDIO bus at 1.8 V, as stock runs it (`sdio-used-1v8`): port G is at 3.3 V and the
      bus at high-speed. DLDO1 feeds port G and the eMMC alike, so find stock's switch first.
- [ ] SD at UHS speeds: stock claims SDR50, DDR50 and SDR104 at 150 MHz, its I/O on DLDO1 at 3.3 V
      or ALDO1 at 1.8 V through two `vqmmc*sw` supplies; mainline takes one `vqmmc` that switches.
      Find how VCC-PF moves between the rails first.
- [ ] Give the tree the PA and PL banks' supplies, `vcc-pa` and `vcc-pl`: both fall back to dummy
      regulators.
- [ ] A regulator for USB0's VBUS: `usb_phy_generic` refuses a dummy for it.
