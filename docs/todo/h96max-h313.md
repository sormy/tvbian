# H96 Max H313: what is left

## Needs the owner

- [ ] USB 2.0 at 480M: a stick in each USB-A port in turn, then the port nearest the SD slot (OTG)
      as a host; `fio` measures each.
- [ ] The remote: press every button against `rc-h96max-h313`'s 22 keys.
- [ ] The BLE remote: pair it with `pair-remote`, then check it reconnects after a reboot.
- [ ] SD hotplug: a card out and back in while running.
- [ ] Composite on 6.18: PAL colour bars watched on the AV jack, as on 7.3. The writeback reads them
      back intact on both kernels; nobody has watched the picture.
- [ ] Whether the FEL button in the AV jack reaches Linux: watch PH9, the factory tree's
      `recovery_key`, with `gpiomon` while it is pressed. If it does, it becomes `KEY_VENDOR` on
      `/dev/input/recovery-button`; the board tree has no key now.
- [ ] The box boots from the eMMC after the FEL route, with a release image carrying U-Boot `0008`.

## Unattended

- [ ] On 7.3, what was run on 6.18 only: ten warm reboots and an all-CPU lockup.
- [ ] Read the backup's eMMC back through the factory-settings FEL loader and match it.
- [ ] Heat: four loaded cores reach the 60 and 70 °C passive trips, peak at 73 °C and settle at
      1200–1320 MHz, so `cpu.load.cooling` is known in `known.conf`. The Tanix's stock trips are the
      same; a larger heat sink, or the cooling maps, would keep the cores unthrottled.
- [ ] The Wi-Fi's SDIO bus at 1.8 V, as stock runs it (`sdio-used-1v8`): port G is at 3.3 V and the
      bus at high-speed. DLDO1 feeds port G and the eMMC alike, so find stock's switch first.
- [ ] SD at UHS speeds: stock claims SDR50, DDR50 and SDR104 at 150 MHz, its I/O on DLDO1 at 3.3 V
      or ALDO1 at 1.8 V through two `vqmmc*sw` supplies; mainline takes one `vqmmc` that switches.
      Find how VCC-PF moves between the rails first.
- [ ] Give the tree the PA and PL banks' supplies, `vcc-pa` and `vcc-pl`: both fall back to dummy
      regulators.
- [ ] A regulator for USB0's VBUS: `usb_phy_generic` refuses a dummy for it.

- [ ] **s2idle needs `pm_async=off`.** With async resume the box hangs late in resume, every time
      since the tree changes after `d3bb7be`; serial resume wakes 8 of 8. Some device resumes before
      one it needs. Not the media blocks, USB hosts, I2C3, SDIO or Ethernet; left: display, eMMC,
      the timers. Find it, give the tree the missing dependency, drop the parameter.
- [ ] **The watchdog resets a hang in suspend.** A device stuck 200 s in its noirq suspend
      (`tools/validate/sleep-stall`) left the box dead until power-cycled on 6.18.63, where the
      Tanix, same SoC and `0092`, resets in 131 s. Awake, unpinged, it resets in 129 s.
