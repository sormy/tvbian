# SWT6621S: what is left

The Seekwave Wi-Fi/BT combo's open items, on every board that carries it.

- [ ] **The chip does what stock does**: access point, Wi-Fi Direct, its interface modes.

- [ ] **Wi-Fi keeps working after hours of traffic.** Once, on the H96 Max M2 Plus, connects stalled
      after 2 h until a reboot.

- [ ] **Throughput falls to about 14 Mbit/s for minutes, then recovers past 200.** On the H96 Max
      3518D, 2026-11-05, on 5 GHz channel 40 at a 540 Mbit/s link rate, a 4K HDR stream starved for
      3.5 to 31 s at a time while the driver logged `skw_work_process: setup TXBA failed, ret: -1`
      (block-ack setup) and, every 10 s, `SKW_CMD_GET_STA expect len: 385, recv len: 172`. The
      driver gives `iw dev wlan0 link` no station data; `nmcli dev wifi` has the rate. Next: the
      rate and the TXBA failures logged together through a slow spell.

- [ ] **Wi-Fi is at full speed at once after resume.** It takes about a minute today.

- [ ] **The driver can reset its chip**, beside `sdio-pwrseq`, which holds the reset GPIO today.
